You don't need a telescope to get started.

## What you need

- Dark sky (get away from city lights)
- A **red** flashlight
- Patience — eyes need ~20 minutes to adapt

## Easy targets

1. **The Moon** — obviously
2. **Jupiter** — bright, steady, doesn't twinkle
3. **The Pleiades** — a tiny cluster, looks like a mini dipper
4. **Andromeda Galaxy** — 2.5 million light-years away[^1]

## Light-year math

One light-year is about `9.46 × 10^12` km. That's *a lot* of kilometers.

[^1]: Which means the light you see left it before humans existed.
