Last weekend I hiked to the summit. Here's a quick recap.

## The Route

| Segment        | Distance | Elevation gain |
| -------------- | -------: | -------------: |
| Trailhead → Hut |   6.2 km |          650 m |
| Hut → Ridge     |   3.1 km |          480 m |
| Ridge → Summit  |   1.4 km |          210 m |
