### Waves

The waves come in sets. You wait, and then:

> One.
>
> Two.
>
> > Three — and the big one arrives.

### Things I noticed

* The water is colder than it looks
* Seagulls are **fearless**
  * especially around sandwiches
  * and chips
* Sunsets last longer when you don't check your phone
