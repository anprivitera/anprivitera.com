A short guide to wandering a city without a plan.

#### Rule #1: Walk

Seriously. Walk everywhere. Use `maps` only when you're truly lost.

#### Rule #2: Eat local

1. Find the busiest small place
2. Point at whatever the person next to you ordered
3. Enjoy

#### Rule #3: Look up

Most people never look above the first floor. The architecture up there is *fantastic*.
