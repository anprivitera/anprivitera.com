My current pour-over recipe.

## Ingredients

| Item   | Amount |
| :----- | :----: |
| Coffee |  15 g  |
| Water  | 250 g  |
| Temp   | 94 °C  |

## Steps

1. Rinse the filter with hot water
2. Add coffee, make a small well in the middle
3. **Bloom**: pour 40 g of water, wait 30 s
4. Pour the rest in slow circles
   - Don't hit the filter walls
   - Aim to finish by `2:30`
5. Enjoy ☕

> **Tip:** grind size matters more than the brewer.
