Three days in the desert taught me a few things.

## 1. Water is everything

Carry more than you think. Then carry a bit more.

## 2. Silence is loud

Without traffic or notifications, you start to hear *everything*: wind, sand, your own heartbeat.

## 3. Nights are cold

| Time  | Temperature |
| ----- | ----------: |
| 14:00 |       41 °C |
| 22:00 |       18 °C |
| 04:00 |        6 °C |
